SOUNDS IN THIS FOLDER / SONIDOS DE ESTA CARPETA
===============================================

ENGLISH
-------
Every file here is a real recording of a mechanical keyboard switch,
taken from two open-source projects and adapted for Aura: one keystroke
was cut out (a letter key for "on", the space bar for "off"), trimmed,
and brought to the same volume as the others. Nothing was synthesized.

From Mechvibes (https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes), MIT License,
Copyright (c) 2021 Hai Nguyen:
  cream_*.wav     NK Cream (sound pack "NK Cream (original by Ryan)")
  oreo_*.wav      Everglide Oreo
  crystal_*.wav   Everglide Crystal Purple
  topre_*.wav     Topre (purple, hybrid, PBT keycaps)
  mxblack_*.wav   Cherry MX Black (PBT keycaps)
  mxred_*.wav     Cherry MX Red (PBT keycaps)
  mxbrown_*.wav   Cherry MX Brown (PBT keycaps)
  mxblue_*.wav    Cherry MX Blue (PBT keycaps)

From kbsim (https://github.com/tplai/kbsim), MIT License,
Copyright (c) Thomas Lai:
  holypanda_*.wav Holy Panda
  blackink_*.wav  Gateron Black Ink
  buckling_*.wav  Buckling spring
  alps_*.wav      SKCM Blue Alps
  boxnavy_*.wav   Kailh Box Navy

Switch and keyboard names belong to their owners and are used only to
say what was recorded. Aura is not affiliated with any of them.

ESPAÑOL
-------
Cada archivo de esta carpeta es una grabación real de un switch de
teclado mecánico, tomada de dos proyectos de código abierto y adaptada
para Aura: se recortó una pulsación (una letra para "on", la barra
espaciadora para "off"), se ajustó el inicio y se igualó el volumen con
el resto. No hay nada sintetizado.

La lista de archivos y su origen es la de arriba. Los nombres de los
switches y teclados pertenecen a sus dueños y se usan solo para decir
qué se grabó. Aura no está afiliada a ninguno de ellos.
